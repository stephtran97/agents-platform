# Aurora Analytics Product FAQ

## Plans and pricing
Aurora has three plans. The Starter plan costs 29 dollars per month and includes 3 users and 10 dashboards. The Team plan costs 99 dollars per month and includes 25 users and unlimited dashboards. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing, single sign-on and a 99.9 percent uptime guarantee.

## Refunds
Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly subscriptions are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the billing period.

## Data retention
Raw event data is retained for 13 months on Starter and Team plans and for 36 months on Enterprise. Deleted dashboards can be restored within 14 days.

## Integrations
Aurora connects to PostgreSQL, BigQuery, Snowflake and Google Sheets. Webhooks are available on the Team and Enterprise plans. The REST API is limited to 600 requests per minute per workspace.

## Support
Starter customers get email support with a one business day response time. Team customers get chat support during business hours. Enterprise customers have a named support engineer and a 1 hour response time for critical issues.
