Information Security Policy

Passwords and access
All employees must use a password manager and multi-factor authentication. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Shared accounts are not allowed. Access to production systems is reviewed every 90 days and removed on the last day of employment.

Incident response
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery. The security team assigns a severity from SEV1 to SEV4. SEV1 incidents trigger an immediate all-hands call, and a post-incident review is published within 5 business days.

Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data is always Restricted and must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Restricted data may not be copied to laptops or personal cloud storage.

Devices
Company laptops use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately so they can be remotely wiped.

Vendor reviews
New vendors that handle Confidential or Restricted data need a security review before a contract is signed. Reviews are repeated every year.
