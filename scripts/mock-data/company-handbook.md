# Northwind Employee Handbook

## Vacation and leave
Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid vacation per year. Unused vacation days can be carried over for one calendar year. Sick leave is separate and is capped at 10 days per year. Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for all parents.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head. The home office stipend is 400 dollars, paid once.

## Expenses
Submit expense reports within 30 days of purchase through the finance portal. Purchases above 500 dollars need written approval from a manager before they are made. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day.

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Outside core hours, employees choose their own schedule. On-call engineers receive one day of compensatory leave for each week on call.
